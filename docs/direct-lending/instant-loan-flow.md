# Instant Loan Flow & Decisioning

## 1. Fast-Track Loan Origination
The instant loan flow minimizes borrower drop-off by removing unnecessary friction:
- Real-time pre-qualification calculator evaluates desired amount and tenure against product eligibility.
- Auto-completes KYC and Bank accounts if verified on profile.
- Automated BRE Decision Engine generates instant outcomes within seconds:
  - `APPROVE`: Generates binding Offer with Statutory KFS.
  - `REFER`: Safe borrower message ("Under review by credit underwriting team").
  - `REJECT`: Safe customer-facing reason without leaking internal risk weights.

---

## 2. Statutory Key Fact Statement (KFS)
Every generated offer contains:
- Total Loan Principal
- Upfront Deductions (Processing Fees + GST + Documentation Charges)
- Net Disbursed Amount
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
- Monthly EMI and Repayment Schedule
- Late Payment Charges and Grace Period
- Cooling-off Period & Grievance Officer Details
