# Borrower Digital Journey

## 1. End-to-End Consumer Journey

```
1. Registration & Consent
   │ ─ Customer provides mobile/email OTP
   │ ─ Grants digital consents (KYC, Bureau, Terms)
   ▼
2. Profile & Employment
   │ ─ Personal identity, address, employment type, monthly income
   │ ─ Auto-fills from verified store for returning users
   ▼
3. Digital eKYC
   │ ─ Aadhaar OTP / Digilocker verification
   │ ─ PAN verification with ITD database
   ▼
4. Bank Account Verification
   │ ─ Penny drop validation
   │ ─ Direct disbursement routing
   ▼
5. Instant Pre-qualification & Decision
   │ ─ Automated BRE credit rule execution
   │ ─ Risk grade & anomaly scoring
   ▼
6. Transparent Offer & KFS
   │ ─ Approved principal, net disbursement, APR, fees, EMI schedule
   │ ─ Single-click digital acceptance
   ▼
7. Digital Agreement (eSign)
   │ ─ Electronic signature stamping
   ▼
8. eNACH Auto-Debit Mandate
   │ ─ Debit card / Netbanking setup for scheduled EMIs
   ▼
9. Instant Disbursement
   │ ─ Direct IMPS payout to verified bank account
   ▼
10. Active Servicing & Repayment
   │ ─ Real-time schedule tracking, payment links, auto-debits
   ▼
11. Repeat Borrowing & Limit Reassessment
   │ ─ Zero-DPD on-time payment track record unlocks higher limits
```

---

## 2. Borrower UX Rules
- **Clarity First**: Always display exact net disbursement and APR before asking for acceptance.
- **Stage Progression**: Clearly communicate current stage, completed stages, and next steps.
- **Single-Click Actions**: Next valid action is highlighted as a primary banner on the borrower home screen.
