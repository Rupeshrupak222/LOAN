# Executive Command Center Guide (`/command-center`)

## 1. Operating Cockpit Overview
The Command Center is reserved exclusively for senior management (`SUPER_ADMIN`, `COMPANY_ADMIN`, `ADMIN`, `FINANCE_CONTROLLER`, `RISK_MANAGER`). It provides a high-level operating view of institution-wide performance without cluttering the screen with raw transactional queues.

---

## 2. Telemetry Pillars
1. **Enterprise Snapshot**:
   - Total AUM, Daily originations velocity, Approval conversion rate, Disbursement outflow, Collection efficiency, Gross NPA, and Overall Portfolio Risk status.
2. **Growth Velocity**:
   - 7-day trailing velocity of applications submitted and loan disbursements.
3. **Credit & Underwriting Bottlenecks**:
   - Auto-decisioning conversion rate, committee queue depth, and average sanction TAT.
4. **Risk & Fraud Anomaly Monitoring**:
   - Real-time detection of approval limit clustering, sudden branch rejection rate spikes, and gateway latency circuit-breakers.
5. **Human-in-the-loop Governance**:
   - Direct escalation and resolution triggers with mandatory audit justification notes.
